Free Age & Date of Birth Calculator: Exact Years, Months & Days

Welcome to the ultimate Age Calculator tool! Whether you need to find out your exact chronological age for job applications, official documents, visa forms, or simply out of curiosity, our precision calculator computes years, months, weeks, days, hours, and minutes instantly based on your date of birth.

Key Features of Our Age Calculator

1. Absolute Precision: Accounts accurately for leap years, differing month lengths (28, 30, or 31 days), and seasonal calendar variations.

2. Custom Target Dates: Calculate your exact age as of today or choose any past or future reference date to see what your age was or will be.

3. Granular Time Breakdowns: View your existence broken down into total months, weeks, days, hours, and even minutes lived.

4. Next Birthday Countdown: Instantly check how many months and days are left until your next birthday celebration.

How to Use the Age Finder

Step 1: Select your exact Date of Birth from the calendar picker.

Step 2: Verify or change the Calculate Age As Of date (defaults to today's date automatically).

Step 3: Click Calculate Age to view your detailed chronological breakdown instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How does the calculator handle leap years?

Our algorithm automatically factors in leap years (every 4 years where February has 29 days) to ensure your calculated age in days and hours is 100% precise.

2. Can I calculate someone else's age or past dates?

Yes! By adjusting the "Calculate Age As Of" field, you can calculate historical ages, project future age milestones, or find the age difference between any two dates.

3. Is this age calculator free to use?

Completely free with no registration or downloads required. It runs fully in your web browser.
